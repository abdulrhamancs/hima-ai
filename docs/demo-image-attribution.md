# Demo report image attribution

These photographs are bundled only for Hima AI's static demo/sample reports. They are representative imagery and are not evidence of the sample report's displayed place, time, or incident. Real Supabase reports must use their persisted user-uploaded `image_url` instead.

## `report_tree_cutting.jpg`

- Representative use: tree/wood removal demo.
- Original: [Jubail, Saudi Arabia by Mountain, 1935 23.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jubail,_Saudi_Arabia_by_Mountain,_1935_23.jpg)
- Description: Bedouin man with a camel loaded with wood, Jubail (Al Jubayl), Saudi Arabia, 1935.
- Creator/source: Joseph D. Mountain; National Air and Space Museum Archives.
- License: public domain in Saudi Arabia (`PD-Saudi Arabia` on Wikimedia Commons).
- Change: downloaded at a reduced resolution for the Android resource.
